Relay (Talking R-3) satellite operations

*** Description ***

The relay satellite is used as a stop-gap measure while advanced construction is being researched. The satellite is installed on top of a modified R-3 800 rocket, with the upper stage functioning as satellite. The satellite is fully reusable and mission parameters leave an allowance for maneuvering and deorbit.

*** Launch procedure ***

Mission is only defined in the equatorial plane

Initial boost is done with a Winchester (30 m/s, 30 deg turn) launch
Launch vertically until 30 m/s, then turn east until 30 degrees of vertical
Rocket is stabilized using lower stage fins from lower stage
Stage at lower stage MECO
Enable SAS
***UPPER STAGE REQUIRES ACTIVE STABILIZATION***
Continue with nose pointing to prograde direction, manually correcting to follow velocity vector
Cut throttle when apoapsis reaches 75Km
Circularise at 75Km
At proper moment, inject into a 75x750 transfer orbit
Circularise when apoapsis is reached

***BEWARE FALSE DV READINGS***
In some cases, dV reported is incorrect. Satellite has enough dV to reach work orbit and have a 400 to 500 dV reserve for deorbiting.

*** Deorbit procedure ***

Start at the 750 Km circular orbit used for operations
Select landing site
Locate point at 80 degrees past landing site
Take a retrograde attitude
Execute retro burn until periapsis altitude is 40 Km
Disable all attitude control
Near periapsis, take prograde attitude and then release SAS
Satellite will skip through atmosphere
Next atmospheric entry should be close to landing point
Satellite will re-enter atmosphere head towards surface
	Consider rocking the satellite to expedite speed bleed
Drogue chutes should open at about 8 Km altitude and fully deploy at 5 Km
Main chutes should follow
