Talking R-3 relay assembly instructions

Assembly has been divided in modules to take advantage of multiple KCT rates.

** Engine section (fresh start) **

*** Part list ***

1x	LV-909 "Terrier" Liquid Fuel Engine
1x	FL-T400 Fuel Tank
2x	Communotron 16-S
18x	OX-STAT Photovoltaic Panels

*** Assembly ***

Start with T400 tank
Install Terrier engine axially below tank
Install C-16S radios radially on tank
	2x symmetry
	Direction E/W
	Antenna touching "rivet ring panel" on top and checkerboard panel (NOT the checkerboard itself) at the bottom
Install 6x solar panels radially on tank
	2x symmetry
	Direction N/S
	Longer side vertical
	Top panel touching edge of "rivet ring panel"
	Install other in a vertical row immediately before preceding panel
Install 12x solar panels radially on tank
	4x symmetry
	Direction NE/SE/NW/SW
	Longer side vertical
	Top panel touching edge of "rivet ring panel"
	Install other in a vertical row immediately before preceding panel		

Panel design should leave a gap for radio sets.


** Engine section (javelin conversion) **

*** Part list ***

1x	RS-2 Javelin upper stage (any variant)
18x	OX-STAT Photovoltaic Panels

*** Assembly ***

Start with RS-2 stage
Discard fairing and decoupler
Set aside computer module and service bay for bay and computer assembly
Install 6x solar panels radially on tank
	2x symmetry
	Direction N/S
	Longer side vertical
	Top panel touching edge of "rivet ring panel"
	Install other in a vertical row immediately before preceding panel
Install 12x solar panels radially on tank
	4x symmetry
	Direction NE/SE/NW/SW
	Longer side vertical
	Top panel touching edge of "rivet ring panel"
	Install other in a vertical row immediately before preceding panel

** Bay section **

*** Part list ***

2x	Service Bay (1.25m)

Take the service bay from the RS-2 for use in this section

*** Assembly ***

Stack two bays one on top of the other axially

If bay is taken from RS-2, the bay should go on the top, with its computer module as-is.

** Computer module (fresh start) **

*** Part list ***

1x	Probodobodyne OKTO
1x	Z-200 Rechareable Battery Bank
1x	Mk16 Parachute
2x	Communotron 16

*** Assembly ***

Start with OKTO computer
Install Z-200 bank axially on top of computer
Install parachute axially on top of battery
Install C16 radially on OKTO
	2x symmetry
	Direction E/W
	Centre of OKTO panel
	Depress into OKTO until wide part cannot be seen
	Antennas should be contained in payload bay after final assembly

** Computer module (RS-2 conversion) **

*** Part list ***

1x	RS-2 computer module
2x	Communotron 16

*** Assembly ***

Start with computer module
Install C16 radially on OKTO
	2x symmetry
	Direction E/W
	Centre of OKTO panel
	Depress into OKTO until wide part cannot be seen
	Antennas should be contained in payload bay after final assembly

** Power module **

*** Part list ***

3x	Z-200 Rechareable Battery Bank
6x	Z-100 Rechareable Battery Pack (optional)

*** Assembly ***

Start with Z-200 battery bank
Install 2 Z-200 banks axially on top and bottom of initial bank
Optionally, install Z-100 packs radially on battery stack
	6x symmetry
	Direction E/W/NNE/NNW/SSE/SSW
	Bottom of battery pack aligned with bottom of stack

** Final assembly **

The power module tends to have the longest manufacturing time. The engine tends to have the shortest time because of familiarity with the parts.

*** Parts list ***

Modules may be modified or assembled in different order (e.g. bays integrated with engine module). Bay modules tend to take more time (KCT only) that would be expected considering its simplicity.

1x	RS-1B rocket stage
1x	Engine module
1x	Bay module
1x	Computer module
1x	Power module
1x	Aerodynamic Nose Cone

*** Final Assembly ***

Start with engine module
Install bay module axially on top of engine module
Install computer module on upper bay floor
	Parachute should extend past the top of the stack
Install power module on lower bay floor
Install nose cone on top of bays
	Ensure cone is affixed to upper bay
	Cone should cover parachute

When assembly is complete, install axially on top of RS-1B stage.
Re-root to upper bay.
