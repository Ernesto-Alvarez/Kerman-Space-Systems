Model 1123 relay satellite

***Description***

The model 1123 satellite is an HG-5 based relay satellite that takes advantage of the wide angle of the HG-5 antenna to cover a large footprint on planetary surfaces. The presence of two extra antennas allows the satellite to act as a multi network relay or to have redundant communications.
