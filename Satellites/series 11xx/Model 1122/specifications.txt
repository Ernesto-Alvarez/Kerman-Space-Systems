Model 1122 relay satellite

***Description***

The model 1122 satellite is an HG-5 based relay satellite that takes advantage of the wide angle of the HG-5 antenna to cover a large footprint on planetary surfaces. The presence of a second antenna allows the satellite to relay to off-world targets, to act as a close range (Mun to Kerbin) relay or as part of a larger communications network.
