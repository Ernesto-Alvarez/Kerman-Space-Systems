Model 1301 simple temperature/pressure probe

***Description***

The model 1301 simple probe for close exploration of Mun/Minmus. The short range comms of the probe requires the use a launch-flyby-return mission with the RemoteTech computer to execute the science operations out of range of communication networks. In-range operations are possible if a relay satellite is in orbit of the bodies to investigate.

Data is transmitted in the "return" phase when close to Kerbin. The probe can be retasked after transmitting the science data.
