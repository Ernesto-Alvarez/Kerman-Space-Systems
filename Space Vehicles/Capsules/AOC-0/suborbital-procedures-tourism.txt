****NORMAL PROCEDURES****

Pre flight

Computer hibernate		OFF
Radios				ON
Computer EC			FULL
Pod EC				FULL
O2 tank				FULL
LFOX Tanks			FULL
Decoupler			STAGEABLE
Pod Parachutes			CHECK PARAMS (0.6/1200/10)
Rocket Parachutes		CHECK PARAMS (0.6/1000/10)
Throttle			MAX

Launch
Set KER to surface + land
	Landing pointer shows estimated landing position
	Perform pre-flight checklist

Rotate and tilt rocket to guide landing indicator to desired landing point
	Rocket will veer towards hatch side
	Hatch-side forces increase until shortly before MECO
	Tilt rocket after MECO for final adjustments
	Landing point can be placed within a 25Km circle around KSC

At 70 KM
	Stage

Capsule lands automatically upon reentering atmosphere.
