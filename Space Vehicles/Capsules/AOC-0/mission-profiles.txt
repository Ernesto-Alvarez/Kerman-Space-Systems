AOC-0

Suborbital AOC-0 launch profile

Specifications:
Launcher	R-1
Max altitude	105Km
Vertical flight

Equipment loadout:
None

Mission plan

**Final assembly**
Mount AOC-0 capsule on top of decoupler.
	Parachutes should align to R-1 parachutes
	Hatch should align with R-1 gas generator exhaust
	Hatch should align with R-1 fin
Set capsule parachutes to stage 0.
	Activate when booster decoupler and booster parachutes are activated
Orient capsule/booster stack with capsule hatch pointing towards desired direction of gravity turn
	Usually east
	AOC-0 causes booster to tilt towards hatch side
Re-root to Mk1 Command pod

**Launch**
Move to launchpad.
Set throttle to maximum.
Stage engine until all propellants burn up.
At 70 Km altitude, stage coupler and parachutes.
Capsule and rocket return passively to surface.

Mission variants
	Exploration
	Tourism

Notes
	Most issues are mitigated by ejecting capsule from booster. Capsule re-enters passively when not attached.


Orbital AOC-0 equatorial orbital launch profile

Specifications:
Launcher	R-2A
Max altitude	100x100Km

Equipment loadout:
EVA pack
Parachute

Mission plan

**Final assembly**

Mount AOC-0 capsule on top of decoupler
	Parachutes should align to R-2 main parachutes
	Hatch should align with R-2 side with fin and drogue chute
Set capsule parachutes to stage 1
	Activate when upper stage parachutes are activated
	That happens before the decoupler is staged
Orient capsule/booster stack with capsule hatch pointing towards desired direction of gravity turn
	East, in all but very few exceptions
	AOC-9 causes booster to tilt towards hatch side
Re-root to Mk1 Command pod


**Launch**
Move to launchpadSet throttle to maximum
Stage engine
Wait until speed is 50 m/s
Turn until reaching 30 degrees from vertical
Raise apoapsis to 100 Km
Circularise at apoapsis

**Re-entry**
Take retrograde attitude
Burn until fuel exhaustion
Eject service module
Automatic re-entry

Mission variants
	Exploration
	Tourism

Use of AOC-0 is not recommended in orbits other than equatorial (or close to equatorial). Use of AOC-0 in high inclinations is FORBIDDEN for tourism flights.
