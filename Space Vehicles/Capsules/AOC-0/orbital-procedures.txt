**Launch

Set KER to orbit + heat (if available)
Wait for vertical velocity to reach 50m/s
Tilt in E direction to 30 degrees off vertical for equatorial	OR
	Tilt in appropriate direction to 25 degrees off vertical for other inclinations
Eject lower stage booster at propellant exhaustion
Keep burn until apoapsis is at 100Km for equatorial launches	OR
	Keer burn until apoapsis is at 80Km for other inclinations

**Pre-orbital injection

Execute after SM engine cutoff after launch. Should be complete before loss-off-comms from KSC

LFOX levels		CHECK
	Ensure enough propellants are left for an orbital insertion plus 50m/s dV return, abort insertion otherwise
After passing 40Km altitude
	Stage 1			ACTIVATE
		Parachutes should remain active during rest of flight in case of unexpected re-entry
RemoteTech users should program insertion burn at this point and hand it over to flight computer
RemoteTech users MAY issue an "Attitude OFF" command to happen after burn at this point

**Orbital insertion

Attitude			PROGRADE
Burn until circular orbit is established
	RemoteTech users should use flight computer to execute insertion burn as insertion point is usually beyond KSC reach

**Orbital operations

If possible, when not maneuvering
	Computer		HIBERNATE
For trained crews
	Radios			CONSIDER 1 ON/1 OFF or 2 OFF for increased endurance
		Ships with only tourists on board MUST keep both radios active

**Re-entry

Re-entry may be programmed into flight computer for RemoteTech users. ENSURE THAT THERE IS ENOUGHT TIME SO THAT FULL PROGRAM IS SENT.

Set attitude to RETROGRADE
Burn SM engine until propellant exhaustion
One re-entry confirmed
	Stage 0			ACTIVATE
		Capsule and service module should separate
Flight computer attitude	OFF

Capsule should perform re-entry automatically (and passively)

****EMERGENCY PROCEDURES****

**** DURING LAUNCH ****

**** Mid Launch Abort (MLA)

Proceed with launch until ejection of lower stage (booster)
	In certain emergency cases, staging may proceed during lower stage boost, risking lower stage damage
		Throttle	CUT
		Staging		ACTIVATE

At booster MECO
	Throttle		CUT
	Stage 2			ACTIVATE

Kerbal Engineer			LANDING MODE

If necessary, execute burn to move estimated landing point away from hazardous terrain (e.g. mountains)

Once secure landing point is found
	Throttle		CUT
	Stage 1			ACTIVATE
	Stage 0			ACTIVATE
	If decoupler fails to stage
		Heat shield	JETTISON
		Execute ALA return if heat shield fails to jettison

Capsule should perform re-entry and landing passively
Rocket stages should land safely

Note: If stage separation fails, perform stage separation failure checklist

**** Attached launch abort (ALA)

This abort mode is used when dealing with multiple failures, including a failure to detach the capsule from the service module

BELOW 18Km and 750m/s

Stage 1				ACTIVATE
Drogue parachutes will deploy
Capsule and service module should land automatically


ABOVE 18Km or faster than 750m/s

Attitude			PROGRADE

Until apoapsis is 35Km
	Throttle		FULL
	Burn to get AP above 30Km
	
Throttle			CUT

Staging				ACTIVATE ALL STAGES UNTIL STAGE 1
	Make sure to enable parachutes

At apoapsis
	Attitude		RETROGRADE
	Throttle		FULL
		Bleed as much horizontal speed as possible

Once horizontal speed is bled
	Throttle		CUT
	Attitude		RETROGRADE
		Maintain retrograde attitude for as long as possible
		Maintain attitude as far away from prograde as possible until parachute opening
		If engines are needed for attitude control, apply minimum thrust to keep attitude

When drogue parachutes open
	Attitude		CEASE ALL CONTROL

Capsule and service module should land automatically

**** IN ORBIT ****

**** Attached re-entry and landing (ARL)

This re-entry mode is used when a capsule cannot or should not detach from the service module

Attitude			RETROGRADE
SM engine			BURN until PE is 50 Km
Staging				ACTIVATE PARACHUTES
	Do not activate TD-12 decoupler

If battery power is high (>50%)
	Fly through 55 to 45 Km band of the atmosphere
	Attempt to minimise vertical velocity
	Bleed excess horizontal velocity
	When battery power is low (50%) or craft is forced to drop below 45Km, stop flying
		Attitude	RETROGRADE

Maintain retrograde attitude for as long as possible

At 45Km altitude
	Throttle 		MAXIMMU
		Execute a retrograde burn to bleed excess speed and lose excess mass

When forced to depart from retrograde attitude, actively attempt to maintain attitude as close to retrograde as possible

When drogue parachutes deploy
	Attitude		CEASE CONTROL INPUTS

Capsule and service module land automatically attached to each other

**** Capsule wheel failure

Wait until acceptable re-entry point is reached
Thrust				MINIMUM
Attitude			RETROGRADE
	Use SM engine gimbal to maneuver in place of wheels
When retrograde attitude is reached
	Thrust			MAXIMUM

Continue with normal re-entry procedures

**** Capsule battery failure

Execute normal re-entry as soon as safe to do so

**** Capsule storage leak

Execute normal re-entry as soon as safe to do so

**** Computer battery failure

Execute normal re-entry as soon as suitable re-entry point is reached

**** Capsule parachute failure (x1)

Conserve battery power
Execute re-entry procedure as soon as mission objectives complete
	Expedite re-entry on low battery power
	Consider ARL if dual parachute damage is suspected
	Favour normal re-entry is battery power is low and dual parachute damage is not probable

**** Capsule parachute failure (x2)

Consider
	WAIT FOR RESCUE

If re-entry is necessary or imminent
	Execute ARL re-entry procedure as soon as safe landing point is reached

ARL requires active control during re-entry to ensure survival of crew and that fact should be taken into account before executing ARL

**** Capsule radio failure

If able crew is on board
	Proceed with mission
If tourist is on board
	Execute normal re-entry as soon as safe point is reached

**** Heat shield ejector failure

Attempt to activate decoupler
If failed
	Execute ARL procedure


**** Service module engine failure

WAIT FOR RESCUE
	Situation is not deemed survivable without outside help

**** Service module LFOX leak

Execute normal re-entry as soon as suitable re-entry point is reached

**** Service module parachute failure

Execute normal re-entry as soon as suitable re-entry point is reached

**** Service module decoupler failure

If deorbit burn complete
	Heat shield			JETTISON
	Execute normal re-entry

Decoupler MUST NOT be staged in orbit
	Ejection of service module in orbit is NOT SURVIVABLE

**** Improper ejection of service module

WAIT FOR RESCUE
	Situation is not survivable without outside help
