**Important assembly instruction**
To make an effective use of the R-12 tanks, they are mounted axially on the cubic strut that is used to mount the parachute on the K-1. The tank mounting uses the same trick that allows the creation of a new axial point where the parachute is mounted. The tank mounts on the lower axial node present in the cubic strut.
This trick allows the tanks to "wrap around" the K-1 body.

**Assembly**

Start with fully assembled K-1 kicker
Remove 4x OX-STAT panels
	Keep handy for later use
Install R-12 tanks
	Take an R-12 tank (tank 1)and slide upwards along the body until it locks up on the OKTO computer.
	Slide tanks 2, 3 and 4 in the same way, locking them on the preceding tank
Install OX-STAT panels radially over R-12 tanks
	4x symmetry with snap
	Long side of panels vertically (following direction of thrust)
	Mount on tank 2
		Orient in direction of thrust using rotation tool
		Directions N,S,E,W
