**Assembly**

Start with fully assembled K-1 kicker
Remove 4x OX-STAT panels
	Keep handy for later use
Install R-11 tanks radially over OSCAR-B tanks
	4x symmetry with snap
	Top of tanks should be aligned with top of OKTO computer
Install OX-STAT panels radially over R-11 tanks
	4x symmetry with snap
		Each tank should get 1 panel
	Long side of panels vertically (following direction of thrust)
	Centred between structural elements of the R-11 tanks
