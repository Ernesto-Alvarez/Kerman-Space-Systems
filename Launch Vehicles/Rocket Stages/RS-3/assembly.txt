RS-3 Rhino Lower stage

**Uses**

R-4 launch vehicle

**Technology levels**


**Parts list**
1x	TVR-1180C Mk1 Stack Tri Coupler
12x	FL-T400 Fuel Tanks
3x	LV-T30 "Reliant" Liquid Fuel Engine
3x	Mk-55 "Thud" Liquid Fuel Engine
3x	EAS-4 Strut Connector
3x	Mk2-R Radial-Mount Parachute
1x	TD-12 Decoupler

**Assembly**
Start with a TVR-1180C Mk1 Stack Tri Coupler
Install fuel tanks axially below tricoupler
	3x symmetry to populate all 3 stacks
	Stack 4 levels high for a total of 12 tanks
	Lower tanks should have a checkerboard pattern
Install Reliant engines axially below the tank stacks
	3x symmetry to poulate all stacks
	Exhaust facing outwards
Install Thud engines radially on each tank stack
	3x symmetry to attach to all 3 stacks
	Outward position on the stacks (right above the reliant gas generator exhaust)
	As low as possible, lower part of housing at the point where the Reliant engine tapers in
Install struts
	3x symmetry
	Connect on lower band in checkerbox on lowest tanks
	Line up a pair of tanks until they are symmetrical, connect on second black square counting from the space between the two tanks
Install parachutes radially
	3x symmetry, one on each stack
	As high as possible in uppermost tank, concave part at limit, tips touching tricoupler
Install decoupler axially on top of tricoupler

Fairing may be substituted for a C2 type for smaller payloads.

**Notes**
Stage rated for water landing only. Landing on ground may result in damage.
Tri coupler causes thrust assymmetry. Rocket tends to pivot towards side with "battery" sign (square with two dots) in green paint scheme or towards "panel" side on others.
