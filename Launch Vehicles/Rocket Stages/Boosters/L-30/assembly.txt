L-30 Reliant based liquid fuel booster

Part list

1x	FL-T400 Fuel Tank
1x	FL-T100 Fuel Tank	(L-30 500 only)
1x	LV-T30 "Reliant" Liquid Fuel Engine
1x	Aerodynamic Nose Cone
1x	Mk2-R Radial-Mount Parachute

Assembly procedure

Start with T400 tank
Install T100 tank axially on top of T400 tank (L-30 500 only)
Install Reliant engine axially at bottom of tank
	Gas generator exhaust should point WEST
Install nose cone axially on top of uppermost tank
Install parachute radially on tank
	Use 1x symmetry and snap
	Facing WEST
	Tips of parachute should touch top of T400 tank (top part of rivet ring panel)
Tank priority should be identical for all tanks
