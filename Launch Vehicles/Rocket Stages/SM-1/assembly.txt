SM-1 service module

Parts list
1x	FL-T400 Fuel Tank
1x	LV-909 "Terrier" Liquid Fuel Engine
1x	TD-12 Decoupler
3x	Basic Fin
2x	Mk2-R Radial-Mount Parachute
2x	Mk12-R Radial-Mount Drogue Chute

Assembly procedure
Start with Terrier engine
Mount T400 tank axially on top of engine
Mount TD-12 axially on top of tank
Mount fins radially on tank
	3x symmetry with snap
	W/SE/NE direction
	As low as possible (centre fin rivet should lay in line at centre of checkerboard)
Mount Mk12 drogue chutes radially on tank
	2x symmetry with snap
	E/W direction
	Centre of parachute at lower junction of "rivet ring" panel
	Configuration
		Altitude 5000
		Pressure 0.03
		Angle 10
Mount Mk2 parachuts radially on tank
	2x symmetry with snap
	N/S direction
	Centre of parachute at lower junction of "rivet ring" panel
	Configuration
		Altitude 1000
		Pressure 0.60
		Angle 10
