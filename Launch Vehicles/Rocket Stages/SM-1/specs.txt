SM-1 Service module for exposed (AOC-0 and AOC-1) capsules

Description

Dumb (no onboard computer) rocket stage used as service module, orbital injection rocket and deorbit rocket for non-maneuvering, exposed, 1.25 cm capsules.
The SM-1 is used as the upper stage of the R-2 launch vehicle and remains attached to the capsule as an orbital maneuvering system. The SM-1 requires power, control and communications that is being provided by the capsule and will not function on its own. Staging is usually set so that the parachutes are activated as part of the jettison maneuver.
The stage is built to allow a spacecraft to passively reach orbit without any type of SAS. Fins provide capsule stabilization during ascent. The fins are designed to burn up on reentry. The rest of the stage is recoverable.
