RS-2 "Javelin" cargo stage

Description:
Low tech, Terrier-based smart launch stage. Recoverable.

Uses:
Upper stage for orbital launch rockets
Orbital injection stage

Variants:
RS-2A	Short fairing version. Fairing A1. Flight computer is an OKTO.
RS-2B	Medium fairing version. Fairing A2. Flight computer is an OKTO.
RS-2C	Long fairing version. Fairing A3. Flight computer is an OKTO.
RS-2D	Improved short fairing version. Fairing A1. Flight computer is a HECS.
RS-2E	Improved medium fairing version. Fairing A2. Flight computer is a HECS.
RS-2F	Improved long fairing version. Fairing A3. Flight computer is a HECS.

Used in:
R-3 series of launch vehicles
