RS-4 Bison Upper stage

**Uses**

R-4 launch vehicle

**Technology levels**


**Parts list**

1x	LV-T45 "Swivel" Liquid Fuel Engine
3x	FL-T400 Fuel Tank
1x	Service Bay (1.25m)
1x	Z-200 Rechargeable Battery Bank
1x	Probodobodyne HECS
1x	C3 Fairing
1x	TD-12 Decoupler
2x	Communotron 16-S
2x	Mk2-R Radial-Mount Parachute

**Assembly**

Start with swivel engine
Stack 3 T400 tanks axially on top of the engine
	Or the last stacked tank
	You should end with a stack of 3 tanks on top of each other
Stack service bay on top of T400 tank
	Doors facing E/W
Install battery axially inside payload bay
	On upper internal axial point 
	Battery hangs from bay's roof
Install computer axially inside payload bay
	On lower internal axial point
	Battery rests on bay's floow
	HECS brand towards W
Install C2 fairing on top of service bay
	Installation may be deferred until final rocket assembly
Install decoupler axially inside floor of fairing
	Only is fairing was installed
Install communotron radios radially on middle tank
	2x symmetry with snap
	Direction E/W
	Exactly in between the top and bottom of tank
Install parachutes radially on exterior of service bay
	Direction N/S
	At the bottom of the bay, concave part touching the bottom, tips going into the tanks
