**Ascent data
Activate SAS
Ascend vertically until v = 50 m/s
Roll to indicated roll program course (do not roll if not indicated)
Initiate pitch over maneuver at max deflection until velocity vector reaches indicated value with SAS active
While pitching, nudge rocket sideways to reach POM course
Once POM is complete, hold prograde
	For block 2, switch to SAS prograde ASAP
	For block 1, disable SAS and let fins hold attitude
		Enable SAS after LS MECO and hold prograde manually with SAS assistance
	If using RemoteTech, set flight computer to prorade surface and switch to orbital at 38 Km altitude
	For block 2 at high altitude, fine tune orbital inclination by switching between orbital and surface SAS modes
Eject fairing at 70 Km altitude
Burn until apoapsis = 100 Km
Circularise at apoapsis
Eject payload and execute retrograde burn to recover booster
