R-3 1200L assembly procedure

Part list

1x	RS-1E rocket stage (for block 1)	or
1x	RS-1G rocket stage (for block 2)
1x	RS-2C rocket stage (for block 1)	or
1x	RS-2F rocket stage (for block 2)

Assembly procedure

Start with RS-1 rocket stage
Connect RS-2 stage axially on top of RS-1 decoupler
If using DangIt, unload all parts from service bay

Configure staging
Stage 4
	Reliant engine
	Booster engines
Stage 3
	Booster parachutes
	Booster separators
Stage 2
	Terrier engine
	Lower stage parachutes
	Lower decoupler
Stage 1
	Fairing
Stage 0
	Upper stage parachute
	Payload decoupler
