R-3 1200L

Equatorial profile
Launch from:	KSC
Payload mass:	3050 Kg
Orbit:		100 x 100 Km
Return dV:	3.46 * dV with max payload (Estimated 60m/s)

Ascent data
Ascend vertically until v = 15 m/s (if necessary, start turn earlier rather than past the 15m/s mark)
	NOTE: gravity turn can start immediately after takeoff, but course may be unstable
Initiate gravity turn at max deflection until velocity vector reaches 45 degrees from vertical
Eject boosters
	Boosters should eject at about the same time the turn is complete
	Booster ejection should take priority before holding prograde (fins can take care of that for now)
Hold prograde
	First stage can hold by itself because of the fins
	Second stage needs active correction. 
		Use manual correction with SAS on block 1 or HECS prograde on block 2.
		If using RemoteTech, set flight computer to prograde.
Eject fairing at 70 Km altitude
Burn until apoapsis = 100 Km
Circularise at apoapsis
Eject payload and execute retrograde burn to recover booster
NOTE: return from a 100x100 Km orbit requires 30 m/s dV. Remaining dV after orbital insertion burn (before payload release) should be at least 9 m/s. Reduce insertion burn if needed to ensure proper return dV.
