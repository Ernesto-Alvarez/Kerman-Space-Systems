**Ascent data
Ascend vertically until POM start speed (normally 30 m/s)
Initiate pitch over maneuver at max deflection until velocity vector reaches indicated degrees from vertical (normally 25 degrees)
Hold prograde
	First stage can hold by itself because of the fins
	Second stage needs active correction. 
		Use manual correction with SAS running on block 1 or HECS prograde on block 2.
		If using RemoteTech, set flight computer to prograde.
Eject fairing at 70 Km altitude
Burn until apoapsis = 100 Km
Circularise at apoapsis
Eject payload and execute retrograde burn to recover booster
