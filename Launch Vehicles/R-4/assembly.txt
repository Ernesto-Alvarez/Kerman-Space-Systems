R-4

**Part list**
1x	RS-3 lower stage
1x	RS-4 upper stage
3x	TT-18A Launch Stability Enhancer

**Assembly**

Take RS-3 stage
	Rotate so that the "panel" side is facing opposite the direction of turn
		Usually means panel to the WEST
Stack RS-4 stage axially on top of decoupler in RS-3
	Radio antennas should face E/W

Connect launch clamps to triple stack
	Between tanks 2 and 3

**Configuration**

Staging
	Stage 3
		Reliant engines
		Thud engines
		Launch clamps
	Stage 2
		Lower stage decoupler
		Lower stage parachutes
		Swivel engine
	Stage 1
		Fairing
	Stage 0
		Upper stage parachutes
		Upper stage decoupler

**Note**

Point upper part of tricoupler opposite of direction of turn (in most cases, point to west)
Rocket is not rated for ground landing. Stages should ditch in ocean. Minor damage may happen in a ground landing.
