R-1 assembly procedure

Parts list
1x LV-T30 "Reliant" Liquid Fuel Engine
2x FL-T200 Fuel Tank
1x FL-T100 Fuel Tank
3x Basic Fin
2x Mk2-R Radial-Mount Parachute
1x TD-12 Decoupler

Assembly procedure
Start with Reliant engine
	Position gas generator exhaust pointing to the north
Connect T200 tank axially on top of engine
Connect T200 tank axially on top of T200 tank
Connect T100 tank axially on top of upper T200 tank
	Use Dark ("black lines") pattern
Connect decoupler axially on top of T100 tank
Install fins radially on T400 tank
	3x symmetry with snap
	Position N/SE/SW
	As low as possible durinbg fitting
	Lower fin rivet should be positioned at the bottom of the engine shoud "warning pattern"
Install parachutes axially on T100 tank
	2x symmetry with snap
	Position E/W
	Raise parachutes along rocket length until centre of parachute top intersects with bottom of upper line on T100 tank
Configure parachutes
	Min pressure	0.6
	Altitude 	1000
	Angle spread	10
Configure staging
	Stage 1
		Engine
	Stage 0
		Decoupler
		Parachutes
Configure fuel priorities
	Increase bottom tank priority by 2
	Increase mid tank priority by 1
Repaint as necessary
