TM-1 Test Mass assembly

Parts list
1x	Small Holding Tank
1x	Mk0 Liquid Fuel Fuselage
4x	Stratus-V MInified Monopropellant Tank

Assembly procedure
Start with holding tank.
Assemble Mk0 tank axially on top of holding tank.
Install Stratus-V tanks radially on top of holding tank.
	Use 4x symmetry with snap.
	Install so that black band of tank touches Mk0 tank.
	Start installation by pretending to install radially on mk0, then travel down until it installs on top of holding tank. Finally, travel upwards until black band touches Mk0 tank.
Isolate all tanks.
